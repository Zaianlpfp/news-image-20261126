## 1. KIM ORDERS TACTICAL NUKES PUSH — North Korea / security (hero)

- **What:** North Korean state media KCNA reported on Thursday that Kim Jong
  Un, after inspecting nuclear-material production and weapons research
  facilities, called for tactical nuclear weapons to be deployed more widely
  across multiple domains, saying "Our nuclear capability is now a reality".
  Photos released with the report showed gas centrifuges and miniaturised
  Hwasan-31 warheads.
- **Date logic:** the inspection itself was on Wednesday 25 Nov; the KCNA
  report, the photos and the call are Thursday 26 Nov — first reported 26 Nov.
- **Source:** Reuters, dateline "SEOUL, Nov 26" —
  https://www.reuters.com/world/asia-pacific/north-koreas-kim-visits-nuclear-facilities-calls-wider-tactical-nuclear-weapons-2026-11-25/
- **Image wording:** "KIM ORDERS TACTICAL NUKES PUSH" + "After touring
  nuclear sites, Kim Jong Un calls for tactical nuclear weapons to be
  deployed more widely, state media reports." — "calls for" is the reported
  wording; the image does not claim a deployment happened.
